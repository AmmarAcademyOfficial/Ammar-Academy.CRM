# Staff guide

A one-page guide for each role. Share the section that fits each person.

## Sales agents

**Every shift**
1. Open **My Work**. Work the lists from left to right: *Overdue follow-ups* → *Due today* → *Not yet contacted* → *New, not opened yet*, then *Awaiting payment* and *No next action*.
2. In the side bar, **Available for new leads** shows whether you are receiving leads. Use **Pause new leads** for a break; it ends by itself at the end of your shift.

**New leads and returning customers**
- A lead you have not opened yet is highlighted in **yellow** with a **New** label (Leads, My Work). It turns normal as soon as **you** open it; a manager looking at it does not change it. **Leads → Not Opened Yet** lists them.
- A **Returning customer** label means this person had a lead before (same phone number, main or second). New leads from a returning customer come to the agent who handled their last lead, with a notification, as long as you sell that program; you get it even off shift and see it next shift. If you are on leave, it goes to the next agent in turn. The panel lists their earlier leads.

**For each lead**
- Click the name to open the customer panel. Use **Call** or **WhatsApp**, then **Record outcome**. Only a recorded outcome counts as contact (and for your first-contact time in Reports).
- Always leave a **follow-up** (date, time and what to do), or choose why there is none.
- Set the **course** the customer wants (top of the panel). Managers filter and report by course.
- Move the stage as the conversation progresses. **Lost** needs a reason.

**When the customer pays**
1. **Payments** tab → **Start enrollment**. The price starts from the course price; a different price needs a reason (for example "Sibling discount").
2. **Submit payment**: amount, date, method, reference and a photo or PDF of the receipt (not needed for cash).
3. A manager confirms it. You are notified either way. Once the payment rule is met, the customer is **Enrolled** and handed to support automatically.

**Notifications** (everyone)
- The **bell** lists your notifications. Important ones also **pop up** at the bottom of the screen with a short sound while the CRM is open; click **Open** to go to the customer.
- The sound plays only after you have clicked somewhere on the page once. Mute it with the speaker button in the bell.
- **Turn on desktop alerts** (in the bell) to be alerted when the CRM tab is in the background.
- You are reminded before each follow-up, and told when a new lead of yours has not been opened after 30 minutes (managers can change both).

You see only your own leads. You cannot confirm payments or reassign leads.

## Customer support

1. **My Work → New customers**: customers handed over after a confirmed payment. Click **Assign to Me**. If two people click at once, only one gets the customer. A customer a manager assigns to you shows in **yellow** with a **New** label until you open it.
2. Open the customer → **Support** tab: tick each **checklist** step as you do it, add **support follow-ups**, and leave notes.
3. Set the status to **Active** once every checklist step is done (the CRM will not allow it earlier).
4. A customer marked **Flagged** had a payment reversed; a manager will review it.

You see handed-over customers only (the queue and your own), never sales prospects.

## Managers

**Daily**
- **Overview**: new leads, the waiting queue (**Run assignment now**), who is on shift, Google Sheets health, payments to confirm.
- **Payments**: open each receipt, then **Confirm** or **Reject** (with a reason). **Reverse** a confirmed payment that bounced.
- **Needs Review**: sheet rows the CRM could not import safely; fix and import, or dismiss with a reason.

**Weekly**
- **Reports**: choose the dates, program and course type. Every figure has **How it is counted**. **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet.
  - *First contact* counts only the agent's scheduled shift time, so a lead arriving at night is not counted against them.
  - *Enrollments* are counted once, when the qualifying payment is confirmed and the customer is handed over.
  - *Confirmed payments* exclude pending, rejected and reversed payments.
- **Settings → Notifications**: for each type of notification and each role, switch it on or off and choose **Pop-up and sound**. Managers get one for every new lead and for each lead not opened in time. Set the follow-up reminder time and the not-opened alert time (0 turns it off) under **Timing**.
- **Settings → Audit History**: who changed what (roles, reassignments, prices, payment decisions, settings).

**Staff accounts** (*Settings → Users and Roles*):
- **Add user**: name, email, role, then either **Set a password now** (they can sign in straight away) or **Email them an invitation link** (they choose their own password).
- **Set password** on any active person replaces their password immediately, for example when someone forgets it. Passwords need at least 10 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number. Tell people their password in person, never by chat. Each change is recorded in **Audit History** (the password itself is never stored or shown).

**Settings you own**: users and roles, shifts, courses and prices, customer fields, stages, tags, follow-up rules, notifications, payment handover rule and deposit per program, the support checklist, Google Sheets connections.

## Getting help
- Forgot password: ask a manager to **Set password** for you, or use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
- Something looks wrong: note the customer, the time and what you clicked, and tell a manager. Managers can check **Audit History** and the customer's timeline.

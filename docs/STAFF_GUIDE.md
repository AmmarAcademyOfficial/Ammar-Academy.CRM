# Staff guide

A one-page guide for each role. Share the section that fits each person.

## Sales agents

**Every shift**
1. Open **My Work**. Work the lists from left to right: *Overdue follow-ups* → *Due today* → *Not yet contacted* → *New, not opened yet*, then *Awaiting payment* and *No next action*.
2. In the side bar, **Available for new leads** shows whether you are receiving leads. Use **Pause new leads** for a break; it ends by itself at the end of your shift.

**New leads and returning customers**
- A lead you have not opened yet is highlighted in **yellow** with a **New** label (Leads, My Work). It turns normal as soon as **you** open it; a manager looking at it does not change it. **Leads → Not Opened Yet** lists them.
- A **Returning customer** label means this person had a lead before (same phone number, main or second). New leads from a returning customer come to the agent who handled their last lead, with a notification, as long as you sell that program; you get it even off shift and see it next shift. If you are on leave, it goes to the next agent in turn. The panel lists their earlier leads.

**For each lead**
- Click the name to open the customer panel. Use **Call** or **WhatsApp**, then **Record outcome**. Only a recorded outcome counts as contact (and for your first-contact time in Reports).
- Always leave a **follow-up** (date, time and what to do), or choose why there is none.
- Set the **course** the customer wants (top of the panel). Managers filter and report by course.
- Move the stage as the conversation progresses. **Lost** needs a reason.

**When the customer pays**
1. **Payments** tab → **Start enrollment**. The price starts from the course price; a different price needs a reason (for example "Sibling discount").
2. **Record payment**: type (*Deposit*, *Installment* or *Full payment* of the whole balance), amount, date, method, reference and a photo or PDF of the receipt (not needed for cash).
3. As soon as the payments you record meet the payment rule, the customer is **Enrolled** and handed to support; nobody waits for the confirmation. A manager or the accountant then confirms the payment, and you are notified either way. If a payment is rejected and the customer drops below the rule, the customer is flagged for the managers and support.

**More than one course** (same customer)
- A customer can buy a second course, in the same program or another one (for example IELTS for a current SAT student). On the **Payments** tab, click **Add another enrollment**: choose the course, the agreed total and who is credited (you by default). Each course gets its own card with its own total, payments, payment plan and balance; earlier courses are not changed.
- Use **Record payment** on the card of the course being paid. If one transfer pays for several courses, use **Split one transfer**: enter how much goes to each course; they share one reference and one receipt.
- A different child is a different record: add them as their own lead.

**Notifications** (everyone)
- The **bell** lists your notifications. Important ones also **pop up** at the bottom of the screen with a short sound while the CRM is open; click **Open** to go to the customer.
- The sound plays only after you have clicked somewhere on the page once. Mute it with the speaker button in the bell.
- **Turn on desktop alerts** (in the bell) to be alerted when the CRM tab is in the background.
- You are reminded before each follow-up, and told when a new lead of yours has not been opened after 30 minutes (managers can change both).

You see only your own leads. You cannot confirm payments or reassign leads.

## Customer support

1. **My Work → New customers**: customers handed over as soon as a qualifying payment is recorded (confirmation follows). Click **Assign to Me**. If two people click at once, only one gets the customer. A customer a manager assigns to you shows in **yellow** with a **New** label until you open it.
2. Open the customer → **Support** tab: tick each **checklist** step as you do it, add **support follow-ups**, and leave notes.
3. Set the status to **Active** once every checklist step is done (the CRM will not allow it earlier).
4. A customer marked **Flagged** had a payment rejected or reversed and is below the payment rule; a manager will review it.
5. **More than one course**: when one of your customers buys another course, it comes straight to you (not the queue), with a notification. You can add it yourself: **Payments** → **Add another enrollment**, and credit yourself for the sale. If the customer has several courses, choose which one at the top of the **Support** tab; each has its own checklist, teacher and sessions.

**Payments from your customers**
- Each customer shows the **balance left** for each course. Open the customer → **Payments** → **Record payment** on that course's card: choose the type (*Deposit*, *Installment*, *Full payment*), amount, date, method, reference, and attach the receipt screenshot or PDF. A manager or the accountant confirms it, as for sales. **Split one transfer** divides one payment across several courses.
- After a partial payment, fill in **Next payment** (date and amount) so you are reminded. You can also use **Add expected payment** in the **Payment plan**, and change or remove planned payments.
- Each planned payment shows *Upcoming*, *Due today*, *Partly paid*, *Paid* or *Overdue*. Confirmed payments recorded after the plan was set count toward it, earliest date first.
- **Teachers** (Support tab): **Assign teacher** picks the teacher, one of their rates and, if needed, the subject (a customer can have one teacher for Math and another for Reading). **Stop** ends a teacher from today; they stay on record. Group course teachers are shown too; managers assign those.
- **Sessions**: schedule a customer's sessions from their Support tab (**Schedule**, once or weekly), or from the **Sessions** page for group classes. After each session, click **Record** and choose what happened: *Done* (change the hours if it was shorter or longer), *Cancelled by the student* (tick *late* if it was less than 6 hours before), *Cancelled by the teacher*, or *The student did not attend*. The form shows the hours that will be paid. **Needs recording** on the Sessions page lists earlier sessions you have not recorded yet; teachers are paid from what you record. Private customers show their package hours used and left, and you are notified when 2 hours or fewer are left. A weekly series shows as one line ("Weekly · Tuesdays 5:00 PM · 4 of 6 left"). To move or remove sessions of a series, open one with **Record** and choose **Only this session**, **This and the following** or **All not yet recorded** before changing the date and time (each session moves by the same amount) or clicking **Remove**. Recorded sessions and closed salary months are never changed. Package hours used before the customer was added to the CRM count in the package bar.
- **My Work → Payments due** lists overdue payments and those due in the next 7 days. You get a notification the day before a payment is due, and the morning after if it is not paid (then weekly). Managers are told about overdue payments too.
- **Customers**: every enrolled customer, one row per enrollment, showing course, enrolled date, sales agent, support owner, status, paid, balance, next payment and teacher. **Mine and the queue** (your customers and those waiting to be claimed) or **All customers**. **Filters** sets program, course type, course, enrolled dates, course start dates, status, payments (fully paid, balance left, overdue, waiting for review, no payment yet), sales agent, support owner, teacher and currency. Every filter you set must match; within one group any ticked option matches. Each filter shows as a chip you can remove; your last filters are remembered in your browser. Click a column title to sort, and a row to open the customer.
- **Add existing customer** (Customers page; support and managers): for someone already enrolled before the CRM or outside the sales pipeline. Enter the phone first: if the person is already in the CRM, the course is added to their record (the same student), beside their other courses; a different child gets their own record. Then the course, agreed total (a reason if it differs from the price), enrolled date, the sales agent to credit (or *Before the CRM*), support owner and status; the payments already received (date paid, method, type, optional reference and receipt); what is left (due dates and amounts); and optionally the teacher and package hours already used. Everything is saved together or not at all. Support's past payments go to a manager or the accountant to confirm; a manager's are confirmed at once. These customers get no new-lead alerts, and Reports do not count them as new sales.

You see handed-over customers only (the queue and your own), never sales prospects.

## Accountant

The accountant sees everything in the CRM, read-only: **Leads**, every customer record (phone numbers, timeline, notes, enrollments, payments, teachers and sessions), **Customers**, **Sessions**, **Payments** and **Reports**, and the **Audit history**. The accountant records and reviews payments and reviews enrollments, but never changes leads, stages, owners, notes or settings. Nothing waits for the accountant: customers move to support as soon as a qualifying payment is recorded.

**To review** (Accounting): every new, additional and changed enrollment, with its price (marked **Price differs** when it is not the course price), who entered it and who is credited, plus how many payments wait for confirmation. Click **Reviewed** when it is right, or **Query…** with a note: the person who entered it, the person credited and the managers are told. Queried items stay in their own list until you mark them reviewed. You can also review from the enrollment's card in the customer record.

**Recording payments**: open any customer → **Payments** → **Record payment** on the course's card (or **Split one transfer**). Your payments are confirmed at once.

1. Choose the period at the top: *Today*, *This week* (from Sunday), *This month*, *This Academy month* (26th to 25th), *Last Academy month*, or custom dates. Narrow by program, course type, course, sales agent, support agent or payment method.
2. **Overview**: money in (confirmed payments, by the date the customer paid), payments waiting for review, what customers still owe, and overdue planned payments; money in per day; totals by course type, payment type, program and method. EGP and USD are never added together.
3. **Payments**: open a receipt, then **Confirm** or **Reject** payments waiting for review; click any payment to see it in full or **Reverse** one that bounced. **Download CSV** for Excel.
4. **Customers**: the same customer list support and managers use: agreed total, paid, balance left, next planned payment and overdue amount, with filters that combine and totals per currency. A customer with two courses has two rows; the count shows how many people that is. **Download Excel** saves exactly what the filters show as an Excel file with three sheets (*Enrollments*, *Payments*, *Payment plan*); the Leads list has **Download Excel** too. Each download is recorded in the audit history.
5. **Sales agents**: confirmed money per agent (credited to the agent who made the sale, later installments included), number of payments, new enrollments, and a per-day table.
6. **Teachers**: salaries per Academy month (26th to 25th, named after the month it ends in, so *November 2026* is 26 Oct – 25 Nov). Each teacher's pay is the hours support recorded × the hourly rate on each session's day (a rate change mid-month shows as two lines; a group class is paid once). Use the arrows to change month. **Bonus or deduction** (or **Add** on a teacher) adds a line with a reason, for example *Extra mock exam*; remove it with the bin icon while the month is open. A warning shows past sessions that are not recorded yet, because they are not counted. **Export CSV** for Excel. Totals are per currency.

You are notified when a payment is submitted for review (it names the course) and when a planned payment is overdue.

## Managers

**Daily**
- **Overview**: new leads, the waiting queue (**Run assignment now**), who is on shift, Google Sheets health, payments to confirm.
- **Payments**: open each receipt, then **Confirm** or **Reject** (with a reason). **Reverse** a confirmed payment that bounced. Each payment shows its type and who recorded it (sales or support). Payments you record are confirmed at once. Customers move to support as soon as a qualifying payment is recorded; a rejected or reversed payment that takes them below the rule flags them.
- **More than one course**: a customer can hold several enrollments (any program). **Payments** → **Add another enrollment**, choose who is credited; each course has its own card, payments, plan and handover. **Split one transfer** divides one payment across courses. Reports count each enrollment under its course's program, with *additional enrollments* shown separately.
- **Accounting → To review**: you and the accountant review new, additional and changed enrollments (**Reviewed** or **Query…**); it never holds anything up.
- **Accounting**: the same views as the accountant, plus clicking a customer opens their full record.
- **Accounting → Teachers → Close month** (after the 25th): checks every session is recorded, then freezes that month's salaries. Sessions in a closed month can no longer change, and bonuses and deductions are locked. **Reopen** (with a reason) if something must be corrected; the reason is kept in the audit history.
- **Support → Payments due**: overdue and upcoming planned payments for every support agent's customers (filter by agent with the owner filter).
- **Needs Review**: sheet rows the CRM could not import safely; fix and import, or dismiss with a reason.
- **Moving leads** (Leads list):
  - *Picked leads:* tick leads (the box at the top ticks the whole page; then **Select all … matching** takes every lead matching the filters, up to 500). Click **Move…** and choose **Give them to one agent**, **Share across several agents** (handed out in turn, oldest first) or **Return them to the queue**, with a reason.
  - *One agent's leads:* **Move leads** at the top: choose the agent, optionally a program and created dates (open leads only by default), then give them to one agent, share them, **Swap with another agent** (their matching leads come back the other way) or return them to the queue.
  - Each move is all or nothing. Open follow-ups move with the lead, the timeline says who moved it and why, each agent gets one notification, and the audit history keeps one line. The dialog shows each receiving agent's open leads and warns when a cap would be passed. Who is credited for a past sale never changes. Enrolled, lost and invalid leads can go to an agent but not back to the queue.

- **Download Excel** (Leads list and Customers list): saves exactly what the current filters show, up to 20,000 rows, as a real Excel file: dates are Excel dates (Cairo time), amounts are numbers, the header row stays in place with filter arrows, and money sheets end with a total per currency. The Leads file has one sheet; the Customers file has *Enrollments*, *Payments* (every payment with its type, method, status and who recorded and confirmed it) and *Payment plan*. The accountant can download customers (no phone numbers); support and sales cannot download. Every download is in **Settings → Audit History**: who, when, which list, which filters and how many rows.

**Weekly**
- **Reports**: choose the dates, program and course type. Every figure has **How it is counted**. **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet.
  - *First contact* counts only the agent's scheduled shift time, so a lead arriving at night is not counted against them.
  - *Enrollments* are counted once, when the qualifying payment is confirmed and the customer is handed over.
  - *Confirmed payments* exclude pending, rejected and reversed payments.
- **Settings → Teachers and Rates**: add teachers (programs they teach, contact details) and their hourly rates (for example *SAT private* 400 EGP, *SAT group* 600 EGP). **Change amount** sets a new amount from a date, usually the next 26th; hours before it keep the old amount. Archive a rate instead of deleting it. **Assign to a group course** sets the group's teacher. Each change is in the audit history.
- **Sessions** and **Settings → Teachers and Rates → Session rules**: see every session; set when a student cancellation counts as late (6 hours by default) and whether late cancellations and no-shows are paid (both yes by default). Changes apply to sessions recorded from then on.
- **Settings → Notifications**: for each type of notification and each role, switch it on or off and choose **Pop-up and sound**. Managers get one for every new lead and for each lead not opened in time. Set the follow-up reminder time and the not-opened alert time (0 turns it off) under **Timing**.
- **Settings → Audit History**: who changed what (roles, reassignments, prices, payment decisions, settings) and every Excel download.

**Staff accounts** (*Settings → Users and Roles*):
- **Add user**: name, email, role (*Accountant* sees everything read-only, records and reviews payments, and reviews enrollments), then either **Set a password now** (they can sign in straight away) or **Email them an invitation link** (they choose their own password).
- **Set password** on any active person replaces their password immediately, for example when someone forgets it. Passwords need at least 10 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number. Tell people their password in person, never by chat. Each change is recorded in **Audit History** (the password itself is never stored or shown).

**Settings you own**: users and roles, shifts, courses and prices, customer fields, stages, tags, follow-up rules, notifications, payment handover rule and deposit per program, the support checklist, Google Sheets connections.

## Getting help
- Forgot password: ask a manager to **Set password** for you, or use **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page.
- Something looks wrong: note the customer, the time and what you clicked, and tell a manager. Managers can check **Audit History** and the customer's timeline.

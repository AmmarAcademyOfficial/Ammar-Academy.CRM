## Approved decisions

- Stack: Next.js + React + TypeScript + Tailwind, Supabase PostgreSQL/Auth/Storage, Google Sheets API.
- Data chain: Contact → Inquiry → Enrollment → Support onboarding. One contact can have many inquiries.
- Three roles: Manager, Sales Agent, Customer Support. Permissions are enforced by row-level security and checked database functions.
- Round-robin per program; eligible agents are active, on shift for that program (Cairo time), not on leave, and not paused.
- Only manager-confirmed payments count. One onboarding record per enrollment. Atomic support claim.
- Hosting direction: Vercel + Supabase Pro with `pg_cron` (purchase pending approval).
- Google Sheets connects by **Google sign-in + Google's file picker** (`drive.file` permission), not by sharing sheets with a service-account email (approved with Phase 2). See [GOOGLE_SHEETS_CONNECTION.md](GOOGLE_SHEETS_CONNECTION.md).

## Phase 4 — what was built

Database (2 new migrations; 69 checked functions in total):

- **Courses and prices:**
  - *Programs and Cohorts* became a **course catalog**. Each course has: program, course type, name, price (or "price on request"), details, delivery (in person / online / hybrid), sessions, hours per session, weeks, schedule, start and end dates, capacity, and open/closed.
  - **Course types** (Group course, Private 1:1, Intensive, Mock exams to start) are managed by the manager, with a colour each. They can be archived but never deleted, so old records keep their meaning.
  - Price changes are written to the audit history.
  - Any lead or customer can be given a course from the same program. A closed course cannot be picked for new leads, and the change shows in the lead's activity.
- **Enrollment and payments:**
  - One enrollment per inquiry. The agreed total starts from the course price; a different price needs a written reason (for example "Sibling discount").
  - A payment needs amount, date, method (cash, InstaPay, bank transfer, Vodafone Cash, card, other) and a reference. A receipt is required for everything except cash. Over-payment is refused.
  - **Only payments a manager confirms count.** Totals show agreed, confirmed paid, pending (not counted) and balance.
  - Starting an enrollment moves the lead to Awaiting Payment.
  - **Reject** needs a reason, and the agent is told. **Reverse** (a confirmed payment that bounced) needs a reason too.
- **Handover to support:**
  - When confirmed payments reach the handover rule (full payment, or the program's qualifying deposit), the inquiry becomes **Enrolled**. Exactly one onboarding record is created, with the checklist copied from the template and due dates set, and support and the sales owner are notified.
  - A reversal that takes a handed-over customer below the rule **flags** them for review. They cannot be marked Active until the flag is resolved, and a later confirmed payment clears it automatically.
- **Support onboarding:**
  - A support queue. **Assign to Me** is atomic: when two people click at once, one wins and the other is told.
  - A manager can assign or reassign (a reason is required to reassign).
  - Checklist steps can be ticked and reopened. Status runs New Customer → Onboarding → Active, and **Active needs every step done**.
  - Support follow-ups and support notes are kept separate from sales follow-ups.
- **Permissions:**
  - Support staff see only handed-over customers (the queue and their own), never sales prospects.
  - Sales agents see payments on their own leads.
  - Only managers confirm, reject, reverse, manage courses and edit the checklist.
- **Receipts:**
  - Stored privately; never a public link. Every download goes through the CRM, which checks that the person can see that customer.
  - Each file is checked by its contents, not its name: a text file renamed `.png` is refused. Allowed: JPG, PNG, WebP or PDF up to 10 MB.
  - An upload is tied to the person who made it and expires after 6 hours, so one person cannot attach another person's upload.

App:

- **Settings → Courses and Prices:**
  - Course list with search and filters by program, course type and open/closed.
  - Add or edit a course with all its details, and close or reopen it.
  - Course types with colours.
- **Leads:**
  - New **Course** column (name and price).
  - Filters for **Course type**, **Course** and **No course yet**, which can also be saved as views.
  - A course can be chosen when adding a customer.
- **Customer panel:**
  - Course picker in the header.
  - **Payments** tab: enrollment summary, progress bar, the handover rule in plain words, Submit payment with receipt upload, and payment history with receipt links and the status of each payment.
  - **Support** tab: owner, Assign to Me, status, checklist, follow-ups, and the review flag.
- **Payments (manager):**
  - Payments to confirm, Confirmed, and Rejected and reversed.
  - Stats for this month.
  - A banner listing customers flagged after a reversal.
- **Support staff:**
  - **My Work:** new-customer queue with Assign to Me, due today, and my customers.
  - **Customers:** search and filter by status, owner and flagged.
  - **Follow-ups.**
  - Managers see the same list as **Support**.
- **Settings → Onboarding Checklist:** add, rename, reorder and archive steps, each with due days. Changes apply to customers handed over afterwards.
- **Overview:** "Payments to confirm" and flagged-customer counts.

## Phase 3 — what was built

Database (2 new migrations; 45 checked functions in total):

- **Shifts and distribution:**
  - Weekly shifts per agent with programs, in Cairo time. Overnight shifts run past midnight, and daylight saving is handled.
  - Leave and days off.
  - A rotation pointer per program.
  - Eligibility checks, in this order: active sales agent → not on leave → on a shift covering this program now → not paused → under their open-lead cap (when caps are on).
  - Round-robin per program. Skipped agents keep their place, and manual reassignment never moves the turn.
  - The system assigns an inquiry at most once, enforced with a row lock.
  - With nobody eligible, leads wait in the manager queue and are assigned oldest-first on later runs. Managers are told at most hourly.
  - A pause ends automatically when the agent's shift ends.
  - A manager-added customer is assigned by round-robin immediately. A customer an agent adds stays with that agent.
  - Deactivating an agent asks whether to keep their open leads or redistribute them; follow-ups move with each lead.
- **Google Sheets import:**
  - The Google account's sign-in is stored encrypted, and staff cannot read it.
  - Each sheet connection has its column matching.
  - A row ledger keyed by row identity (never row number) makes every import safe to repeat and safe when two runs overlap.
  - Repeat submissions are logged on the open inquiry. Existing customers get a new inquiry on the same contact.
  - Bad rows go to Needs Review with plain reasons; a manager fixes and imports them, or dismisses them with a reason.
- An automated check over every table: staff can only read, row-level security is on everywhere, and the token column is hidden. Writing this check caught a real gap: new tables inherited Supabase's broad default grants. That was fixed before anything shipped.

App:

- **Settings → Google Sheets Connections:**
  - Connect Google Sheet → Google sign-in (first time) → the file picker opens by itself.
  - Tab and program (program guessed from the sheet name).
  - Automatic English/Arabic column matching, marked "Matched" or "Please check", with a 10-row preview.
  - "New rows only" or "Import existing rows too".
  - Each connection card shows status, last check, last success, totals, Sync now, Edit column matching, Pause/Resume and Remove.
  - A red **Reconnect Google** banner appears when Google access stops.
- **Sync engine and `/api/cron/tick`:** runs every 2 minutes from the scheduler, protected by a secret. It follows moved columns, stops a sheet safely when an essential column is renamed (and heals when it is back), handles 400 new rows per run for long histories, then runs assignment.
- **Needs Review page:** rows that need a person, each with its reason, the original values, **Fix and import**, and **Dismiss** with quick reasons.
- **Settings → Shifts and Distribution:**
  - Who receives leads right now, per program and with the reason when not.
  - A weekly shift grid with add, edit, delete and copy schedule.
  - Round-robin order with up/down controls.
  - Leave.
- **Overview:**
  - Live eligibility per agent and program.
  - Queue with **Run assignment now**.
  - Google Sheets health and a Needs Review count.
- **Local review mode:** a clearly labelled simulated Google, with an English SAT form and an Arabic IELTS form of fictional leads, so the whole flow can be tried without a Google account. `npm run scheduler` imitates the 2-minute scheduler.

## Known limitations

- Google Sheets runs on a simulated Google until the one-time Google setup is approved and done (see [GOOGLE_SHEETS_CONNECTION.md](GOOGLE_SHEETS_CONNECTION.md)).
- The production scheduler (Supabase Cron) needs the Supabase project and your approval (see [SCHEDULER.md](SCHEDULER.md)).
- A sheet row that a manager later fixes in the sheet itself is treated as a new row, because its phone (part of its identity) changed. The old Needs Review item can then be dismissed.
- Receipts are kept in a private folder on the computer in local review; Supabase Storage needs the project.
- An unused receipt upload stays in storage for up to a day before the hourly clean-up removes it.
- The qualifying deposit rule must be set per program by the manager. Until then, handover happens on full payment.
- Card payments are recorded manually; there is no online card payment gateway (not in scope).
- Lists refresh every 60 seconds rather than instantly.
- Not deployed. Backups, monitoring and deployment are prepared and documented but start only when the accounts exist.
- Reports refresh when opened or when filters change; they are not live-updating.
